# ErgoEMA Comprehensive Empirical Evaluation Report

**Dataset**: 14,726 Real-World Front-Facing Video Frames (Primary Scope)  
**Total Multi-Angle Corpus**: 49,113 Frames (Front, Oblique, Lateral)  
**Study Cohort**: 9 De-Identified Participants (`Participant1` through `Participant9`)  
**Methodology**: Online Personalized Calibration + Exponential Moving Average (EMA)  
**Workstation Standard**: ISO 9241-5 Compliant Anterior Desktop Webcam Monitoring  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Results

The **ErgoEMA** architecture provides real-time ergonomic posture monitoring using standard monocular webcams without specialized hardware. It integrates:
1. Scale-invariant front-facing biometrics ($R_{H2S}$ normalized by biacromial span),
2. Rapid 3-second online personalized upright baseline calibration ($\boldsymbol{\mu}_{base}, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_{base}$), and
3. Recursive $O(1)$ multi-channel Exponential Moving Average (EMA) temporal filtering.

On participants held out of tuning (Leave-One-Subject-Out cross-validation), ErgoEMA reaches **70.62% accuracy**, **66.90% F1-score** and a **25.08% False Alarm Rate** across the 9-participant front-facing cohort, with per-participant F1 ranging from 14.43% (Participant 4) to 98.87% (Participant 1). Its EMA update and classification step takes a median of **0.0077 ms** per frame on the machine that generated these results, excluding MediaPipe pose estimation.

All figures in Sections 2–4 are generated by `train.py`; the source of record is [`training_thesis_report.md`](training_thesis_report.md).

ErgoEMA, which scores each frame relative to the participant's own calibrated baseline, has the highest held-out accuracy and F1 and the lowest False Alarm Rate in the table.

## 4. Camera Viewpoint Sensitivity & Perspective Ablation

Evaluated across the complete multi-perspective corpus of **49,113 frames**. Every row uses the deployed hyperparameters ($\alpha = 0.08$, $\delta_{slouch} = 8\%$), which were tuned on the front-view frames, so the Front View row is an in-sample figure:

| Camera Perspective | Viewing Angle | Total Frames | Accuracy (%) | Precision (%) | Recall (%) | F1-Score (%) | False Alarm Rate (FAR) (%) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Front View (Primary Scope)** | $0^\circ$ | **14,726** | **`70.62%`** | **`68.41%`** | **`65.44%`** | **`66.90%`** | **`25.08%`** |
| **Oblique Profile** | $45^\circ$ | 30,827 | 58.11% | 79.46% | 22.28% | 34.80% | 5.80% |
| **Lateral Profile (Spine Biometrics)** | $90^\circ$ | 3,560 | **`96.84%`** | **`94.04%`** | **`100.00%`** | **`96.93%`** | **`6.28%`** |
| **All Combined Perspectives** | All | 49,113 | 57.04% | 64.59% | 26.11% | 37.18% | 13.59% |

### Perspective Analysis:
1. **Front View ($0^\circ$)**: Monocular frontal cameras maintain full visibility of both acromion shoulder landmarks, allowing scale-invariant head-to-shoulder ratio computation.
2. **Oblique Profile ($45^\circ$)**: Suffer from perspective foreshortening that artificially alters apparent biacromial distance.
3. **Lateral Profile ($90^\circ$)**: Excels when utilizing specialized continuous 4-segment dorsal spine estimation (Cervical C7, Thoracic dorsal kyphosis, Lumbar lordosis, Sacral S1) with posterior back offset projection, achieving **96.84% accuracy** and **6.28% FAR**.

## 5. Controlled Pilot Benchmark (Reference Baseline)

For comparative documentation with the initial pilot phase (3,168 frames: the earlier feature extraction of Participants 1, 7, 8, and 9, stored in `data/processed/pilot_dataset.csv`). Scored under LOSO and reproducible with `python train.py --data data/processed/pilot_dataset.csv --output results/pilot --skip-ablation`; the full report is [`pilot/training_thesis_report.md`](pilot/training_thesis_report.md):

| Metric | Static Baseline Model | ErgoEMA (Adaptive + EMA) | Difference |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Accuracy** | 72.73% | **93.06%** | **+20.33 pts** |
| **Precision** | 78.53% | **95.30%** | **+16.77 pts** |
| **Recall** | 59.96% | **90.07%** | **+30.11 pts** |
| **F1-Score** | 68.00% | **92.61%** | **+24.61 pts** |
| **False Alarm Rate (FAR)** | 15.33% | **4.15%** | **72.9% reduction in false alarms** |
| **Processing Latency** | 0.0001 ms | **0.0076 ms** | Measured median per frame, pose estimation excluded |

Tuning and scoring ErgoEMA on the same 3,168 frames gives 94.79% accuracy, 94.52% F1-score and a 3.42% FAR. That in-sample figure was reported for the pilot previously; it is not a validation result.
