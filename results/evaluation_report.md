# ErgoEMA Comprehensive Empirical Evaluation Report

**Dataset**: 13,580 Real-World Front-Facing Video Frames (Primary Scope)
**Total Multi-Angle Corpus**: 47,983 Frames (Front, Oblique, Lateral)
**Study Cohort**: 9 De-Identified Participants (`Participant1` through `Participant9`)
**Methodology**: Online Personalized Calibration + Exponential Moving Average (EMA)
**Workstation Standard**: ISO 9241-5 Compliant Anterior Desktop Webcam Monitoring

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Results

The **ErgoEMA** architecture provides real-time ergonomic posture monitoring using standard monocular webcams without specialized hardware. It integrates:
1. Scale-invariant front-facing biometrics ($R_{H2S}$ normalized by biacromial span),
2. Rapid 3-second online personalized upright baseline calibration ($\boldsymbol{\mu}_{base}, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_{base}$), and
3. Recursive $O(1)$ multi-channel Exponential Moving Average (EMA) temporal filtering.

On participants held out of tuning (Leave-One-Subject-Out cross-validation), ErgoEMA reaches **79.38% accuracy**, **74.05% F1-score** and a **15.67% False Alarm Rate** across the 9-participant front-facing cohort, with per-participant F1 ranging from 14.43% (Participant 4) to 99.39% (Participant 1). Side-on, alerting on the drop from the participant's calibrated upright craniovertebral angle reaches **96.13% accuracy** with a 3.26% FAR. The EMA update and classification step takes a median of **0.0127 ms** per frame on the machine that generated these results, excluding MediaPipe pose estimation. One front-view recording, filmed side-on, is excluded from the dataset (Section 3).

All figures in Sections 2–6 are generated by `train.py`; the source of record is [`training_thesis_report.md`](training_thesis_report.md).

## 2. Benchmark Architecture Comparison (Primary Front-Facing Dataset)

Evaluated across **13,580 front-facing video frames** under LOSO: each participant is predicted by a model tuned or trained on the other eight participants. Latency is the measured median per-frame classification time (pose estimation excluded):

| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **ErgoEMA (Adaptive Time-Series)** | **`79.38%`** | **`76.00%`** | **`72.19%`** | **`74.05%`** | **`15.67%`** | **`0.0127 ms`** |
| Random Forest (100 Trees) | 46.09% | 37.87% | 50.42% | 43.25% | 56.89% | 6.3170 ms |
| Support Vector Machine (RBF) | 55.33% | 45.09% | 44.23% | 44.65% | 37.03% | 0.7080 ms |

> **Key Observation**: The machine-learning baselines, which classify raw feature values, transfer poorly to unseen participants (43–47% held-out F1; Random Forest falls below 50% accuracy). The threshold rule, which scores each frame relative to the participant's own calibrated baseline, has the highest held-out accuracy and F1-score. Logistic Regression has the lowest False Alarm Rate (12.65%) but detects only 36.44% of poor-posture frames, against 72.19% for the threshold rule. The forward-head depth threshold $\delta_{FHP}$ is tuned with $\alpha$ and $\delta_{slouch}$; with it fixed at its former value of 0.06, the same protocol gives 75.15% accuracy, 71.20% F1 and a 25.03% FAR.

## 4. Camera Viewpoint Sensitivity & Perspective Ablation

Evaluated across the complete multi-perspective corpus of **47,983 frames** (47,686 scored). Every row runs the configuration the live app uses: the front-view hyperparameters ($\alpha = 0.08$, $\delta_{slouch} = 8\%$, $\delta_{FHP} = 0.20$) for frontal and oblique frames, and the side-on settings ($\alpha = 0.25$, alert at a CVA drop of $5^\circ$) for frames detected as lateral. The Front and Lateral rows are in-sample (tuned on those frames); the Oblique row is not.

| Camera Perspective | Angle | Frames | Accuracy (%) | Precision (%) | Recall (%) | F1-Score (%) | FAR (%) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Front View (Primary Scope)** | $0^\circ$ | 13,580 | 81.25% | 76.91% | 77.16% | 77.03% | 15.93% |
| **Oblique Profile** | $45^\circ$ | 30,827 | 53.53% | 82.36% | 9.42% | 16.90% | 2.03% |
| **Lateral Profile** | $90^\circ$ | 3,279 | 96.92% | 98.10% | 95.63% | 96.85% | 1.81% |
| **All Combined Perspectives** | All | 47,686 | 61.98% | 70.83% | 33.77% | 45.74% | 12.56% |

With the front-view settings, a $45^\circ$ camera catches only 9.42% of poor-posture frames: the tuned forward-head threshold rarely triggers at that angle. The All Combined row calibrates each participant once on their first upright frames, so side-on frames without a side-on calibration fall back to the clinical $50^\circ$ criterion. The 297 public benchmark images in the lateral dataset carry no participant ID and are not scored.

### Perspective Analysis:
1. **Front View ($0^\circ$)**: Monocular frontal cameras maintain full visibility of both acromion shoulder landmarks, allowing scale-invariant head-to-shoulder ratio computation.
2. **Oblique Profile ($45^\circ$)**: Suffer from perspective foreshortening that artificially alters apparent biacromial distance.
3. **Lateral Profile ($90^\circ$)**: The estimated craniovertebral angle (CVA), with C7 located on the back-of-neck silhouette, is compared with the participant's calibrated upright CVA. Forward Head Posture is named only when the CVA is also below the clinical $50^\circ$ criterion. Thoracic kyphosis cannot be measured from video, so slouch is not assessed in this view.
